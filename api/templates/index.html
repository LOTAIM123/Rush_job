Rules

Goal

Dig a path to the right through endless rock while a wall closes in from the left. The game ends if the wall catches you or you touch lava (a bomb only stuns you, which makes it easier for the wall to catch you). Collect as many points and as much gold as you can while you are alive.

Controls

←↑↓→ or WASDMove ({{ rules.player_speed }} tiles per second)
SpaceMine the block you are facing (works even when you are not lined up with it)
Left mouse buttonMine the block under the cursor (nearby)
EOpen and close the shop (when you are inside a shop room)
1–4Buy an upgrade in the shop
EscBack to the menu
EnterStart a new game
M / F2Sound on / off

The world changes with distance: at the start almost only dirt, around {{ rules.tier_stone }} m mostly stone, from {{ rules.tier_slate }} m almost only slate. Brambles appear from {{ rules.bramble_min }} m, lava from {{ rules.lava_min }} m and bombs from {{ rules.bomb_min }} m, and all of them become more common the further you go.

The Wall and Distance

  • Distance is measured in metres (1 tile = 1 m) from the start; your furthest point counts.
  • The wall's speed is a share of your top speed (base speed plus boots upgrades, not temporary speed power-ups). It starts at {{ rules.wall_start }}, grows by {{ rules.wall_per_100 }} points (of your top speed) every 100 m, and reaches {{ rules.wall_max }} of your top speed at about {{ rules.wall_full_at }} m. It never gets faster than you, but time and boots cannot save you: buying boots makes the wall faster too.
  • The Wall Brake upgrade in the shop is the way to slow the wall down.

Power-ups

Every block you mine yourself has a {{ rules.powerup_chance }} chance (higher with the Luck upgrade) to drop a random power-up. They last {{ rules.powerup_duration }} s each. Only blocks you mine directly can drop one, not blocks destroyed by an explosion.

Shop

Every {{ rules.shop_every }} m there is a shop: an empty room with a counter. Walk in, press E and spend gold on upgrades (the game pauses while you shop). Every price rises by {{ rules.price_growth }} for each 100 m you travel, whether or not you bought anything, and it also goes up each time you buy that item. The prices below are the base prices.

Scoring

Points are awarded for every block that breaks (including blocks broken by 3x3, explosions or bombs). After you die, your score is sent to the server and appears in the table above.
